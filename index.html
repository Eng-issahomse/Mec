01 About

From design intent
to machined part

I am a mechanical engineer from Damascus University working across CAD, CAM and CNC. My hands-on experience is in preparing 3-axis toolpaths in Autodesk PowerMill (roughing, rest machining and finishing) and checking them through simulation and G-code verification.

For my graduation project I designed, assembled and calibrated a 3-axis CNC router, connecting mechanical design in SOLIDWORKS with component selection, GRBL control and G-code motion.

I care about drawings that can be manufactured, toolpaths that are safe and efficient, and tolerances that match the function of the part.

  • Toolpath generation and simulation
  • Machine assembly and calibration
  • Design for manufacturing and assembly
  • Engineering drawings and GD&T
